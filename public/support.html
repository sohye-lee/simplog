Common questions

How do I sync between my phone and computer?
Sign in with the same email on each device (Settings → Account). Your entries and categories sync automatically; it also works offline and catches up when you're back online.

How do I import transactions from my bank?
Download your transactions as CSV, Excel, or a PDF statement from your bank or card website, then open Files → Import. You review every row before anything is saved. Smart import uses AI and is limited to a few imports per month; imports of formats and merchants SimplyLog already knows don't count.

Can I export my data?
Yes — Files → Export as CSV, Excel, or PDF for any month, and Settings → Backup for a full copy.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete account permanently erases your account and all entries. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Is my data private?
SimplyLog shows no ads and never sells your data. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and why.
